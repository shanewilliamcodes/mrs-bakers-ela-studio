How this dashboard works
  • Submissions are one per student per school day. A pencil ✏️ means the student edited after first submitting (students can only edit the same day).
  • Need help counts confidence 1–2. Open one and press Mark checked in after you talk with the student — it stays marked for you.
  • Today's message below lets you replace today's bell work prompt and post an announcement on the student homepage. Clearing the fields and saving removes them.
  • Export CSV downloads whatever the filters currently show.
  • Students choose their period at first sign-in. If one picks wrong, they can change it from their account menu.
