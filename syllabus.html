Welcome

Sixth-grade ELA is a year for building confidence, stamina, independence, and voice. We will read worthwhile texts, discuss big ideas, write for real purposes, and use feedback to improve. Every student begins in a different place; every student is expected and supported to grow.

Core curriculumStudySyncStandardsFlorida B.E.S.T.AssessmentFAST ELA ReadingClass sizeApprox. 15–18

What We Will Learn

  • Analyze plot, character, setting, conflict, theme, and perspective.
  • Determine central ideas and evaluate claims and evidence.
  • Explain how structure, figurative language, and word choice shape meaning.
  • Write organized narrative, informative, and argumentative pieces.
  • Use evidence, elaboration, revision, grammar, and academic vocabulary.
  • Listen, discuss, collaborate, and present clearly.

Our Learning Routine

  1. Connect: a 7 Habits mini-lesson or quickwrite.
  2. Learn: a brief, explicit teacher model.
  3. Practice: reading, writing, or vocabulary in a supported group.
  4. Apply: independent or collaborative StudySync work.
  5. Check: an exit ticket and a clear next step.

Materials

  • Charged district device and headphones
  • Composition notebook or binder section
  • Pencils and a highlighter
  • Independent reading book
  • Schoology access through MySDMC ClassLink

Class Commitments

  • Be ready: arrive, log in, and begin the posted warm-up.
  • Be engaged: read, write, speak, listen, and ask for help.
  • Be respectful: protect everyone's right to learn.
  • Be responsible: use technology and sources honestly.
  • Be persistent: revise, retry, and learn from feedback.

FAST & growth

A score is information, not identity.

FAST progress-monitoring results, classwork, conferences, and short skill checks will help us decide what to practice next. Students will track strengths, choose a goal, practice in small steps, and celebrate measurable growth.

Grades To confirm

Grades will reflect demonstrated learning through reading responses, writing, vocabulary, StudySync assignments, projects, discussions, and assessments. Exact categories and weights will be added after district and school guidance is confirmed.

Late & Missing Work To confirm

Students should communicate early and complete missing learning. The final late-work and reassessment policy will follow Lake Manatee K-8 and School District of Manatee County requirements.

Absences

Check Schoology after an absence, collect missed materials, and ask Mrs. Baker which task comes first. Make-up timelines will follow district policy and individual circumstances.

Academic Integrity

Submit your own thinking and identify outside sources. Translation, spell-check, AI, and other digital tools may only be used as directed. When unsure, ask before using a tool.

Communication

Weekly learning and assignments will appear in Schoology. Mrs. Baker's district email, conference process, and response-time expectations will be added when available.

Family Partnership

Families can help by checking Schoology, supporting regular reading, asking about the current skill, and contacting Mrs. Baker when a student needs a plan.
