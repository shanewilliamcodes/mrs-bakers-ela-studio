    The 7 Habits in ELA

    1Be Proactive Ask for the support you need.
    2Begin With the End in Mind Know the learning goal before you begin.
    3Put First Things First Complete the most important step first.
    4Think Win-Win Build solutions where everyone learns.
    5Seek First to Understand Listen closely before responding.
    6Synergize Combine ideas to create stronger work.
    7Sharpen the Saw Care for your mind and reading life.
