FAST practice that teaches you something

Try a question, explain your evidence, and learn from the feedback. FAST is information for growth, not a label.

Mini practiceCentral idea

Question → Evidence → Choice → Check

How to read FAST this year

Look for movement, not one moment.

PM1Starting point→PM2Midyear check→PM3Year-end result

Families should ask: Which skills improved? Which need another strategy? Is the student moving toward the next achievement level?

The 7 Habits in ELA

Leadership habits become reading, writing, and collaboration habits.

01

Be Proactive

Ask for the support you need.

02

Begin With the End

Know the goal before you begin.

03

First Things First

Do the most important step first.

04

Think Win-Win

Build solutions where everyone learns.

05

Understand First

Listen closely before responding.

06

Synergize

Combine ideas to make stronger work.

07

Sharpen the Saw

Care for your mind and reading life.
