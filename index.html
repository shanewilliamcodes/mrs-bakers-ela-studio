The 7 Habits in ELA

The school emphasizes Stephen Covey's 7 Habits of Highly Effective People. We will connect each habit to reading, writing, teamwork, and personal growth.

01

Be Proactive

I choose my response and ask for the support I need.

02

Begin With the End in Mind

I know the learning goal before I begin.

03

Put First Things First

I tackle the most important reading or writing step first.

04

Think Win-Win

I help build solutions where everyone can learn.

05

Seek First to Understand

I listen closely before I respond or disagree.

06

Synergize

I combine ideas with others to make stronger work.

07

Sharpen the Saw

I care for my mind, body, heart, and reading life.
